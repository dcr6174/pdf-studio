# What we can learn from ihatepdf.cv (reviewed 4 October 2026)

Source: the public homepage of https://www.ihatepdf.cv (text and metadata only; no tools were tested).

## What they do well
1. **One promise, repeated everywhere.** "No watermark, no upload, no sign-up" is in the title, description, social cards and hero. Searchers type "no watermark", so it matches intent.
2. **Comparison pages.** Separate pages for "iLovePDF alternative", "Smallpdf alternative" and "Adobe Acrobat alternative", plus a combined comparison article.
3. **A large guides library.** About 50 "How to … free" articles, one per tool, plus a technical blog explaining how it works.
4. **Use-case angles.** "Edit CV / resume PDF" is called out on the homepage. India-specific tools (GST invoice generator, POS billing) attract local traffic.
5. **Breadth.** Scan to PDF with the phone camera, HTML to PDF, PowerPoint to PDF, Excel/CSV to PDF, PDF to audio, eBook (MOBI/AZW3) to PDF, repair, compare, AI summary.

## Where they slip (avoid this)
- The page says both "60+ tools" and "46 professional tools".
- It says "no file size limits" and, in the same list, "up to 150MB on desktop".
- It says files never leave your device, but its Chat with PDF tool uses Google Gemini.
- Emoji section headings, a meta keywords tag (ignored by Google) and a long block of SEO text under the tools.
PDF Studio's honest limits and consistent numbers are an advantage. Keep them.

## Recommended for PDF Studio (in order)
1. **Title and description:** lead with the searched phrase. Example title: "Free PDF Editor & Tools: No Watermark, No Upload | PDF Studio".
2. **Comparison pages** at `/compare/ilovepdf-alternative`, `/compare/smallpdf-alternative`, `/compare/sejda-alternative`, `/compare/adobe-acrobat-alternative`. Be factual, date them, link to each competitor's pricing page, and say where they are better.
3. **A guides hub** at `/guides` listing every how-to (your tool pages already contain guide text; give them a crawlable index page and a few standalone articles such as "compress PDF to 100 KB", "edit a CV PDF", "sign a PDF on your phone").
4. **Use-case landing pages**: edit resume/CV PDF, compress PDF for government forms and job portals, merge scanned documents, sign a rental agreement.
5. **New tools, only where they can run on the device:** Scan to PDF with the camera (high value on phones), CSV/Excel to PDF tables, HTML to PDF. Treat PowerPoint to PDF and AI chat as later work; label any feature that sends data off the device before the user runs it.
6. **Offline proof:** if the PWA works offline after first load, test it and say so with a short "how to check" note. Don't claim it until tested.
7. **Mascot as differentiator:** use Buddy's expressions on empty, success and error screens; they have nothing similar.
